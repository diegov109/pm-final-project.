# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Persona
- **Role:** Wanderer, a casual subscriber (41% of the base) who watches about 1.1 sessions a week
- **Goal:** Find something worth watching quickly, with no effort
- **Friction:** Scrolls trending rows of mid-tier titles, can’t tell what’s good, and leaves before pressing play

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** Texts to friends, “what to watch” lists and review sites, social media, and a competitor’s curated picks
- **The process:** 1.Open StreamLine, scroll the trending rows, and find nothing convincing.
2.Leave the app to ask a friend or check a “what to watch” list.
3.Search StreamLine to see whether the pick is even available.
4.Start watching, or give up and close the app.
- **Core frustration:** The moment after scrolling when nothing signals what’s good, so they have to leave the app to decide.
- **The evidence:** The data backs it up: 61% of their plays come from trending, searched-then-played fell from 41% to 34%, and only 11% of sessions reach 30+ minutes.

## Future-state journey map
1. Stage 1, Open. Opens the app, sees Spotlight picks first → trusts what’s worth watching right away,  Hopeful but skeptical.  No quality signal
2. Stage 2, Scan. Spots Hidden Gem badges on rail titles → sees quality at a glance , Curious. Can’t tell what’s good
3. Stage 3, Reads the “Why You’ll Love This” reason → chooses in seconds without leaving. Confident ,Decision paralysis
4. Stage 4, Watch and Return. Presses play, then returns for fresh picks → stays past 30 minutes and has a reason to come back, Satisfied. Early drop-off

## Competitive advantages
1.It’s inside the app. The Wanderer doesn’t leave to decide or wonder whether the pick is available.
2.It shows quality at a glance. A hand-picked rail and a badge replace guessing and friends’ texts, and Spotlight cohorts keep 74 to 79% at Month 1 versus 63 to 68%.
3.It takes no effort. The picks refresh without setup, quizzes, or searching, which suits someone who wants zero work to find a show.
