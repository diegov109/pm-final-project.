# GTM Launch Plan, StreamLine (B2C)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Spotlight Curated Rail: a hand-picked homepage rail that bypasses the algorithm. |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Wanderers already subscribe, so awareness isn’t the problem. Only 11% of sessions reach 30+ minutes, and the issue is that they can’t tell what’s worth watching.
- **Target audience:** Primary is Wanderers (41% of the base, about 1.1 sessions a week, 61% of plays from trending). Secondary is Casual Browsers (37%, a 14-point churn improvement when exposed to Spotlight). Power Users are left out of the comms so the guardrail holds.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, The audience is existing subscribers, so there’s no need for press or events. The rail is on the homepage, so the cost of silence is low. The only risk is that Wanderers don’t notice it, or don’t read it as quality, so a light set of comms is enough.

## Channels
1. **Owned: The in-app rail with a short first-session callout, plus an email and push to inactive Wanderers. It reaches the exact audience at zero media cost.**
2. **Earned: Seed the picks with critics and “what to watch” creators. It speaks to the “can’t tell what’s good” problem, and a pick someone else vouches for is easy to share.**
3. **Paid: Retargeting ads on social to lapsed Wanderers and Casual Browsers, using a 15-second clip of the rail.**

## Enablement & Assets
A one-pager for Support on what Spotlight is and how titles are picked, plus macro replies. Assets: in-app callout copy, launch email and push, a 15-second screen recording, and a feature flag for rollout. The gap to flag is an editorial owner to refresh the picks every week.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns the plan and post-launch metrics. Editorial lead owns weekly picks (new ongoing time, so a resourcing ask). Marketing owns email, push, and paid (small test budget needed). Design owns the callout and video. Engineers owns the rollout flag. Support is briefed, with no extra budget. Replace the roles with real names.
- **Timeline:** Weeks 1 to 2 (beta): Run the 50/50 A/B test and seed critics and creators.
Week 3 (launch): If the test meets the ship criteria, roll out to 100%, with in-app callout, email, push, and paid retargeting live.
Week 4 Onward: monitor, collect feedback, and decide on iteration.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** -30+ minute session rate among Wanderers: from 11% to 13% or higher.
-Share of Wanderer plays from curated content: from 18% today, rising.
-Month-1 retention of exposed Wanderers, compared with the 63 to 68% Full Library baseline.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** People click the rail but sessions stay short. That means the picks don’t match what Wanderers want, and the rail is only adding another place to scroll. Also watch for Power User sessions per week dropping below 4.6.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Double down if the 30+ minute rate reaches 13% and the guardrail holds, then extend to Casual Browsers and add A2 and A3. Iterate if the lift is positive but under 2 points. Deprioritize if there’s no lift after the 14-day read.
